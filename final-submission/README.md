## 📌 Overview  
This folder contains the final deliverables for my guided study under Prof. Jung-Eun Chu at CityU.  
The project investigates the **synoptic-scale weather pattern associated with extreme air-pollution days in Mexico City** and compares it conceptually with Hong Kong’s meteorology and health-based indices.

Over six stages, the study evolved from exploratory pollutant comparisons to the development of a **500-hPa anomaly composite system** using NARR reanalysis and a long-term surface dataset (2012–2024).  

The final report includes:  
- Construction of a 13-year pollutant dataset for both cities  
- A climatology-based definition of high-pollution “event days”  
- A corrected 500-hPa composite methodology  
- Interpretation of ridge–stagnation mechanisms over the MCMA  
- A conceptual comparison with Hong Kong's regional synoptic regimes  
- Policy relevance for early warnings and health communication  

The final presentation summarizes the work, key findings, accomplishments, and future research directions.
