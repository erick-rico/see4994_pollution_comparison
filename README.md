## 🌎 Project Overview

This repository contains all analysis, weekly progress, figures, and final deliverables for
my guided study comparing **extreme air-pollution days in Mexico City and Hong Kong**.

The project integrates:
- Long-term pollutant datasets (2012–2024)  
- Mexico City’s **IAyS** and Hong Kong’s **AQHI**  
- Spatial and seasonal patterns  
- A fully corrected **500-hPa NARR composite system**  
- Interpretation of the ridge–stagnation mechanism over the MCMA  
- A conceptual comparison with Hong Kong’s synoptic regimes  

The study combines data analysis, climatology, literature synthesis, and meteorological
interpretation to explain *why* extreme pollution events occur in each city.

## 📊 Data Availability  

The cleaned Mexico City dataset is not included in this repository, not due to access restrictions, but because it required substantial manual processing and consolidation.
The raw data is publicly available through Aire CDMX, and I will happily share the cleaned version upon request.

Hong Kong data is accessible through the **EPD/AQHI** portal.
