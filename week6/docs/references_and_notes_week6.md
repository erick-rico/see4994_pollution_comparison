## Key notes

- **Methodological reference:** composite workflow adapted from a script by a PhD student (Climate Extreme Lab, CityU HK).  
- **Data range:** 2012–2024, using NARR daily pressure-level fields (NOAA PSL).  
- **Focus level:** 500 hPa only; the 850 hPa analysis was postponed due to runtime and debugging constraints.  
- **Results confirm:** a persistent **ridge and subsidence regime** over CDMX during p90 pollutant events.  
- **Comparative insight:** CDMX = home-grown stagnation under a ridge; HK = cyclone-modulated transport and monsoon recirculation.  
- **Interpretation schematic:** “warm bubble” figure illustrating subsidence, inversion, and pollutant trapping over the basin.  
- For further details, figures, and monthly statistics, see the **Week 6 presentation (PDF)**.
