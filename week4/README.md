## 🧠 Key Insights
- **Dust transport (June–July)** raises baseline particulate matter by 2–5×.  
- **NOM-172 (2023)** adopts 1-hour O₃ index → clearer acute-risk communication.  
- **Health impacts:** short-term PM/O₃ spikes → 10 % of CEDVs; long-term PM₂․₅ → ↑ COVID mortality.  
- **Weather patterns:** high-pressure blocking and La Niña years favor stagnation and O₃ episodes.
